### Question 2 (1 point)
**The time of flight for LiDAR is 0.00001 sec. Find the distance of the object from the instrument.**
- [ ] 500 m
- [ ] 1000 m
- [x] 1500 m
- [ ] 2000 m

Yes, the answer is correct.  
(Explanation: distance = c * t / 2 = 3×10^8 m/s * 1e-5 s / 2 = 1500 m.)
